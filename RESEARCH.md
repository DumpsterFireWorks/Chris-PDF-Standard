# Research

Cross-project research and reusable lessons associated with this repository are cataloged in the central private research library:

**`DumpsterFireWorks/Projects-research--library-`**

This repository remains authoritative for its PDF standards/templates and original artifacts. The research library is the cross-project discovery and preservation index; cataloging an item there does not change this repository's authority.

Before archiving or deleting this repository or any research-bearing branch, issue, PR, experiment, or document, verify that reusable research has been cataloged in the library. Do not move credentials, private runtime data, or sensitive evidence into it.
